What a Partnership Actually Gives You

Three things, and the business case for each

Priority access

Your issues and PRs are handled first, under a defined triage SLA, instead of sitting in the public queue. That is what turns “blocked on upstream” into a date you can put in a client plan — and it is why this reads as faster delivery rather than as support.

Roadmap influence

Input on the roadmap and on what goes into a release, so the tools move toward what your clients actually keep asking for rather than away from it.

Credibility

A public listing and logo placement in the partner directory, and a “backed by the author” layer you can put in front of a client during an engagement.

Together these turn upstream maintenance into a predictable layer you can build on: less project risk, fewer internal workarounds, and better margins — because your team stays on high-value consulting work instead of patching tooling.

The program is asynchronous by design, so none of it costs you meetings. Predictable and scalable, so it keeps working as you take on more PostgreSQL work.
