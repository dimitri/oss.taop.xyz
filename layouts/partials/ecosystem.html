Get More Out of the PostgreSQL You Already Run

One learning path, four ways in — from The Art of PostgreSQL

Funded maintenance keeps the tools working. How much you get out of PostgreSQL itself is a different problem — query design, data modelling, and knowing which of its features you are not using. That is what the rest of The Art of PostgreSQL is for.

These aren’t four separate products. They’re one path: start with the book for the foundations, take the course to structure and deepen them, join a masterclass when you want to go further on a specific topic with the author in the room — and license it for the team when it should be everyone’s baseline, not one person’s.
