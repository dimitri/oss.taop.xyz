PostgreSQL adoption continues to accelerate

And with it the need for reliable, repeatable tooling around migrations, data movement, and high availability. Tools such as pgloader, pgcopydb, and pg_auto_failover are already used in production across a wide range of environments—but like most open source infrastructure, their effectiveness depends on timely fixes, predictable releases, and clear operational guarantees.

The Partner Program is designed to provide exactly that: a direct, structured path between your delivery teams and upstream maintenance, ensuring that the tools you rely on behave as expected when it matters.
