Your Clients’ PostgreSQL Migrations Depend on This Code

And on whether anyone is paid to fix it the week you hit a bug

Every PostgreSQL migration, upgrade, and HA setup you deliver runs on tooling somebody has to keep working. pgloader, pgcopydb, and pg_auto_failover are already in production across a wide range of environments — but like most open source infrastructure, what they are worth to you depends on timely fixes, predictable releases, and clear operational guarantees.

The Partner Program is designed to provide exactly that: a direct, structured path between your delivery teams and upstream maintenance, ensuring that the tools you rely on behave as expected when it matters.

What a Partnership Actually Gives You

Three things, and the business case for each

Priority access

Your issues and PRs are handled first, under a defined triage SLA, instead of sitting in the public queue. That is what turns “blocked on upstream” into a date you can put in a client plan — and it is why this reads as faster delivery rather than as support.

Roadmap influence

Input on the roadmap and on what goes into a release, so the tools move toward what your clients actually keep asking for rather than away from it.

Credibility

A public listing and logo placement in the partner directory, and a “backed by the author” layer you can put in front of a client during an engagement.

Together these turn upstream maintenance into a predictable layer you can build on: less project risk, fewer internal workarounds, and better margins — because your team stays on high-value consulting work instead of patching tooling.

The program is asynchronous by design, so none of it costs you meetings. Predictable and scalable, so it keeps working as you take on more PostgreSQL work.

FAQ
What is included in the maintenance subscription?
Our maintenance subscriptions include bug fixes, security updates, performance improvements, and priority support for the covered open source tools.
How is this different from using the software for free?
The software remains freely available under its original open source license. Our subscription provides professional support, guaranteed response times, and funding for continued development.
